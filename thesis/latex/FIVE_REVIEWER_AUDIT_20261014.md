# Five-Reviewer Thesis Audit — 14 October 2026

## Scope and calibration

The report was assessed as a TUM aerospace master's thesis, not against an invented journal target. `criteria_binding_unavailable`: no confirmed journal or venue-specific acceptance criteria were supplied. The five seats below are role-separated analytical perspectives executed in one Codex review context; this is not a claim of independent human or model review.

Review dimensions follow the supplied academic-research framework: originality, methodological rigor, evidence sufficiency, argument coherence, writing quality, literature integration, and significance/impact. The manuscript was also checked for figure references, cross-references, missing graphics, build errors, terminology consistency, and unresolved placeholders.

## Seat 1 — Thesis fit and contribution

**Judgement:** Minor revision before expansion; resolved in the expanded draft.

**Strengths**

- The title, research questions, analysis population, and conclusions refer to the same communication-constrained follow-up problem.
- The literature review connects distributed satellite systems, decentralized allocation, scheduled space networking, and active-fire task construction.
- The contribution is bounded as an architecture trade-space study rather than an operational wildfire-response claim.

**Finding addressed**

- The contribution was present in several chapters but its precise knowledge delta was not consolidated. The added section `Integrated Evidence-to-Decision Synthesis` now distinguishes the methodological, empirical, and decision-support contributions and places them against the four literature areas.

## Seat 2 — Methodology and statistical reporting

**Judgement:** Minor revision before expansion; resolved within the available evidence.

**Strengths**

- Architecture identifiers are paired for the principal regional and fleet-scale comparisons.
- Task hashes, case allow-lists, lower-level recomputation, and balanced-block gates make the final analysis population inspectable.
- Censor-aware timing, clustered bootstrap stability intervals, robustness repeats, exact Pareto filtering, and weight sensitivity are applied to different questions rather than collapsed into one score.

**Finding addressed**

- Uncertainty information was distributed across Methods, Results, Discussion, and appendices. The added `Validity and Uncertainty Budget` page distinguishes architecture variation, event-window variation, failure seeds, base clustering, fixed task assumptions, bundled treatment, and analyst-defined preferences. It states what each procedure supports and what it does not support.

**Arithmetic receipt**

- The report does not rely on a printed t, F, chi-square, or z statistic that can be independently recomputed through the review framework's bounded p-value procedures. The key paired effects and interval limits are instead checked directly against the included CSV outputs.

## Seat 3 — Satellite-systems and domain contribution

**Judgement:** Minor revision before expansion; resolved as a bounded mechanism interpretation.

**Strengths**

- Reception, contact opportunity, transfer event, complete useful-recipient dissemination, and later observation are not conflated.
- The Central Node role is consistently defined as a communication package, not a global scheduler or a general spacecraft class.
- The final results distinguish the verified matched 60/120 block from the balanced India 180-satellite extension.

**Finding addressed**

- Several causal explanations for the high-Central-Node decline were plausible but not uniquely testable from aggregate outputs. The added `Mechanism Interpretation and Competing Explanations` page separates observed package effects from hypotheses about copy exhaustion, contention, placement, eligibility/range, and workload composition. It also specifies the trace or ablation needed to distinguish them.

## Seat 4 — Operational and cross-disciplinary perspective

**Judgement:** Minor revision before expansion; resolved for thesis scope.

**Strengths**

- The report already avoids presenting analyst-defined weights as stakeholder consensus.
- Traffic, differentiated-node burden, ground-segment vulnerability, and robustness are retained alongside mission metrics.

**Finding addressed**

- The practical path from result to design review was implicit. The added `Operational Architecture Decision Path` page assigns decision gates to the task product, forwarding policy, Central Node tier, fleet scale, ground segment, and preference elicitation. It also identifies the mission scientist, communications engineer, spacecraft architect, ground-segment designer, and programme authority as distinct decision owners.

## Seat 5 — Devil's Advocate stress test

**Strongest counter-argument**

The architecture response could be specific to one simulator, one bundled Central Node implementation, one observation-radius proxy, one ground station, and two selected event windows. The report would be vulnerable if it presented these outputs as universal superiority, geographic population effects, or an operationally optimized constellation.

**Adjudication:** Valid against universal claims, but not fatal to the bounded thesis claim.

The added `Adversarial Stress Test and Calibrated Claim Set` page distinguishes supported, suggestive, and unsupported claims. It identifies conditions that would falsify or supersede each supported result. Universal claims are explicitly excluded.

## Editorial synthesis

**Decision after revision:** Submission-ready within the declared master's-thesis scope.

The five seats agreed that the numerical evidence, reproducibility boundary, and conditional conclusions are internally coherent. Their shared concern was not missing simulation output but insufficient consolidation of meaning. Five substantive pages were added to close that gap:

1. contribution and literature positioning;
2. validity and uncertainty budget;
3. mechanism alternatives and discriminating tests;
4. operational architecture decision path; and
5. adversarial claim calibration.

A new data-derived paired-effect figure was generated from the included regional and corrected India fleet-scale CSV outputs. No new simulated result, causal claim, stakeholder preference, or reliability probability was invented.
