\section{Included Machine-Readable Result Files}

Confirmatory evidence is stored under \path{data/final_results}. Its principal files cover the completion gate, matched regional deltas, priority-stratified event times, robustness, exact Pareto membership, rank acceptability, and the conditional five-case shortlist.

The 180-satellite campaign evidence is stored under \path{data/final_corrected_180}; the directory name records its provenance. It includes the verification and case-selection gates, canonical case metrics, Central Node intervals, matched 120-to-180 effects, paired scenario effects, robustness and combined-stress summaries, priority-event tables, exact Pareto membership, rank acceptability, the portfolio extension, and \path{corrected_180_postprocessing_manifest.json}. These compact artefacts are the numerical source of the final report; the multi-gigabyte raw Parquet campaign is retained separately.

The additional fitted-equation, robustness-dispersion, likelihood--consequence, and pairwise-Pareto tables produced for the final review are stored under \path{figures/part2_revision/tables}. The folder includes the full pairwise membership table, not only the four counts printed above.
