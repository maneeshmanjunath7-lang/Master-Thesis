This chapter presents the final Fresh V2 campaign as one connected analysis: integrity and population definition, matched 60/120-satellite results, the India 180-satellite extension, and an integrated decision synthesis. Mission, timing, traffic, and robustness are kept separate so that a gain in one quantity cannot hide deterioration in another.

\section{Fresh V2 Evidence Scope}

The cross-window block contains 54 base geometries and 594 cases per event window, using the intended 32 California or 773 India tasks. The same campaign includes 282 verified India cases at 180 satellites. Aggregate 180-satellite analysis is restricted to the balanced block of 216 cases covering all 27 base architectures at CN30--CN100. This supports a matched India 120-to-180 comparison on those cells, while California--India conclusions remain restricted to 60/120 satellites.
